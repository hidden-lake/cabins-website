Your Cozy Winter Escape on Bear Creek

Eight creekside cabins, a steaming hot tub and sauna under the pines, and Evergreen's frozen lake ten minutes away. Thirty-five minutes from Denver — a world away from it.

Book Your Winter Escape

Winter Is When These Cabins Are at Their Best

Wake to snow on the pines, warm up by the fireplace, and end the day in the shared hot tub and sauna with the creek running below. No lift lines, no crowds — just quiet, mountain, and time to slow down.

Skate the Largest Outdoor Rink in North America

Evergreen Lake sits about ten minutes away — 8.5 acres of Zamboni-groomed ice, night skating, and rental skates at the lakehouse. On December 31, Ice Fest brings food trucks, fire pits, hot chocolate, and broomball to ring in the new year on the ice.

Thaw Out the Way Winter Intends

Every cabin has a fireplace, and the property's shared hot tub and sauna run year-round (6 AM–10 PM). Come in cold from the lake; leave the day behind in the steam.

Red Rocks, Minus the Summer Crowds

The park and its legendary stair workout stay open all winter, and February brings Winter on the Rocks — a rare concert under the stars in the cold. You're a short drive from all of it.

Basecamp for the High Country

On the way to the resorts and close to home base after a day on the mountain. Come back to a warm cabin instead of a hotel hallway.

The Winter Offer

Stay midweek this winter and your third night is on us — Sunday through Thursday, December through March. Book direct for the best rate.
